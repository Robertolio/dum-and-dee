Dum & Dee

Solo (two fingers)

Left thumb = DumRight thumb = Dee

Left thumb = Dum, right thumb = Dee. Drag up to stand, down to bend, push right to walk.

Jump over crates, pallets and the banana peel: flick your thumb up quickly (or tap). Keyboard: Space (Dum), Enter (Dee). Duck on the stairs!

Control:

Rotate your phone to landscape to play Dum & Dee.
